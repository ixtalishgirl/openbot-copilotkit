### 9. Take-over behavior (no constant — structural)

- `agent-computer/src/viewer.ts` claim model: **one cast per Bot**; a second `/stream` **supersedes** (does not close) the first socket. No frame-rate or viewer-count constant exists — N viewers = N casts would each cost a full Chrome encode; currently impossible by design (1 cast max).

Each keystroke/mouse event is one CDP round trip; under latency this is the input-lag lever, and there is nothing to raise — batching would be a code change, not a constant.
